zraX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

zraX is a puzzle game published by griyazilim (www.griyazilim.xyz).

Data we collect

zraX does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. The game does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, stars, sound setting) is saved only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Device sensors

The game reads the accelerometer of your phone to detect tilt so that tiles can fall left or right. This data is used only while you play and is never stored or transmitted.

Third parties

The current version contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking.

Children

zraX does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
